Introduction

We provide advisory and consulting services, business development and sales, creative development services, and digital innovation and business transformation, backed by network expertise, to help enterprises innovate, grow and compete effectively.

We believe that giving enterprises practical, sustainable solutions designed for African markets can unlock new opportunities locally. Together, we are committed to making a positive impact in our communities while supporting enterprise development across the continent.

What we provide

  • Advisory and Consulting Services

  • Business Development and Sales

  • Creative Development Services

  • Digital Innovation and Business Transformation

Our focus is practical: facilitating innovation and business growth while enabling operators to adopt solutions that improve productivity, reduce cost, unlock market access and scale sustainably.

03

Mission & Vision

Mission

Driving sustainable growth through digital innovation and business transformation.

At Emporia, we are dedicated to empowering businesses and communities by fostering innovation and promoting sustainable growth. Our mission is to connect enterprises with market opportunities, enabling them to thrive while addressing the unique needs of the African market and promoting environmentally friendly practices.

Vision

To be Africa’s leading catalyst for sustainable innovation and enterprise growth, transforming communities through digital solutions, clean energy and inclusive economic opportunities.

04

Philosophy

At Emporia, we believe that sustainable progress begins with purposeful digital innovation.

Our philosophy is driven by collaboration, trust and impact: connecting people, technology and ideas to create and scale solutions that empower communities and drive inclusive growth. We view every challenge as an opportunity to build resilience, foster creativity and shape a future where businesses and societies thrive together.

Leadership

Majid Tokurah

Managing Consultant and Digital Transformation Leader

Logistics, Food Systems & Sustainability Innovation Across Emerging Markets

Majid Tokurah is a visionary leader and passionate advocate for digital transformation, sustainable innovation and enterprise support across Africa. As Managing Consultant of Emporia, he brings a wealth of experience in consulting, business development, strategic leadership and ecosystem building.

Through his initiatives, Majid has positioned Emporia as a catalyst for connecting sustainable solutions, from digital transformation tools to clean energy technologies, with real market needs in African communities. His leadership emphasises innovation, inclusivity and impact, ensuring that businesses and individuals have the tools and support to thrive in a dynamic economic landscape.

Driven by a deep commitment to empowering entrepreneurs and fostering long-term growth, Majid champions collaboration, creativity and sustainability as the foundation of Emporia’s mission. His forward-thinking approach continues to inspire teams, partners and communities to embrace innovation, scale responsibly and build a future rooted in shared prosperity and resilience.
